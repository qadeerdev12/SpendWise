# First-time tester exercise (pending)

Use an isolated test account with invented data, never another person's real expenses.
Choose someone who has not worked on SpendWise. Sign them in before timing starts.
Do not explain where the buttons are; record any assistance rather than hiding it.

Give this task: “Record an A$6.80 coffee bought today, in Dining, from Sample Cafe.”
Start timing when the task is given; stop when the app confirms saving. The target
is under 30 seconds after sign-in. Confirm the ledger shows 6.80 with the correct
category/date/merchant. Ask what was confusing. Record observations without names,
credentials or other personal information in the repository.

Optional follow-up: find the expense, change it to A$6.50, and confirm the dashboard
reflects it. Do not time this as part of the original 30-second entry measurement.
